Private league? Add your ESPN cookies

Private leagues need two cookies from ESPN, and only a computer can copy them: log in at fantasy.espn.com, press F12, open Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox), choose Cookies, then espn.com, and copy the values of espn_s2 and SWID. On a phone, choose Type my roster instead.

Works for any fantasy site. We can’t see the other teams in your league this way, so free agents, Game plan, and League need Sleeper.

A big game means 25+ PPR points for QBs, 18+ for RBs and WRs, 14+ for TEs. Strong is a 40% chance or better, Solid is 20 to 39%, and Risky is under 20%. Tags come from the odds alone. The odds also account for the game’s betting lines (how many points each team is expected to score) and, for upcoming outdoor games, forecast wind. The matchup line ranks the opposing defense by fantasy points it allowed to that position over its last 6 games, across all 32 teams. Football is high-variance, so treat all of this as odds, not a promise.
