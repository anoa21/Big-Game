A big game means 25+ PPR points for QBs, 18+ for RBs and WRs, 14+ for TEs. Strong is a 40% chance or better, Solid is 20 to 39%, and Risky is under 20%. Tags come from the odds alone. The matchup line ranks the opposing defense by fantasy points it allowed to that position over its last 6 games, across all 32 teams. Football is high-variance, so treat all of this as odds, not a promise.
